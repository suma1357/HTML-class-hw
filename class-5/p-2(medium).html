Chicken Biryani Recipe

Preparation Time: 1 hour

Servings:4 people.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 2 cups rice
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 tsp black pepper powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan and add cumin seeds, bay leaves, cinnamon stick, cardamom pods, and cloves.
  2. Once the spices are fragrant, add the chicken and cook until it changes color.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.
  4. Stir in the rice and cook for a few minutes.
  5. Add water and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer until the rice is cooked.
  6. Season with salt, black pepper powder, red chili powder, and turmeric powder.
  7. Serve hot with raita or salad.
